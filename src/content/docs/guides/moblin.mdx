[Moblin](https://moblin.app/) has a remote control: an "assistant" can watch and control the
phone while it streams. NOALBS Cloud can be that assistant, so you see its bitrate, bonding and
battery and control it from the dashboard and chat. This is optional and doesn't need OBS open.

Moblin's numbers aren't used for switching: add the server Moblin streams to as a
[stream server](/guides/stream-servers/) for that.

## Connecting

On the switcher's **Settings** page, under **Moblin**, press **Set up Moblin**. NOALBS makes a password
and shows the steps:

1. In Moblin, open **Settings → Remote control** and set the **Password** to the one shown.
2. Open **Streamer → Assistant** and enter the **Assistant URL** shown.
3. Turn **Enabled** on under **Streamer**.

Moblin has one remote control password. If you already use one elsewhere, choose **Use my own
password** and enter it instead. A new password disconnects the Moblin connected now until you
enter it there.

## On the Live page

<Image src={card} alt="The Moblin card: live at 6.4 Mbps, battery, heat, the phone's CPU, a DJI camera, bonding over cellular and WiFi, a Moblink relay, and the controls." width={352} />

The **Moblin** card shows whether it's live, its bitrate and uptime, battery, heat, the phone's
CPU and memory, a connected DJI camera, each bonding connection's share and RTT, and Moblink
relays with their battery.

People who can control the switcher also get: **Go live** / Stop, **Record**, **Mute**,
**Light** (torch), **Stealth** mode, the **scene** and **bitrate preset**, **zoom** (−/+ and
presets), and per bonding connection on/off and priority (with connection priorities turned on).

## Start and stop with OBS

On **Settings → Moblin**, **Start Moblin with the OBS stream** and **Stop Moblin with the OBS
stream** make Moblin go live or stop when the OBS stream does (from OBS, the dashboard, `!start`
/ `!stop`, a raid or a timeout).

## Chat commands

`!mstart`, `!mstop`, `!mbitrate [kbps]`, `!mscene [name]` and `!mstats`. See
[chat commands](/reference/chat-commands/#moblin). They stay silent on switchers without Moblin.
